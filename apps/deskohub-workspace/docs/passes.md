# Cowork passes

An Entry pack pays in advance for five or ten cowork visits at one tier. The
Entry pack prices are in Czech koruna. Deskohub Workspace is not a VAT payer.

| Tier | 5 entries | 10 entries |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Open Space | 1,150 | 2,190 |
| Reserved Desk | 1,590 | 3,090 |
| Reserved Desk with workstation | 2,050 | 3,990 |

The pack is valid from the Prague purchase date. The last day on which a visit
can start is the day before the date two calendar months later for a five-entry
pack, or three calendar months later for a ten-entry pack.

The buyer's eligible discounts apply to the pack price. Calendar sales and
discount-code date restrictions use the Prague purchase date. A paid pass
purchase uses the buyer's first-booking benefit if the buyer applies it. There
is no Dotypos receipt. Billing belongs to the buyer.

Each purchase has its own order. The pack becomes active when payment is
confirmed. It starts with every entry available, and the buyer receives a
confirmation email. An unsuccessful payment releases the discounts it held.
If a late payment can still be applied to the order with the buyer's original
discounts, it activates the pack. A late charge for a superseded attempt, or
one whose discounts can no longer be applied, is marked for refund. For purchases made for someone
else, the buyer provides the holder's sign-in email. Deskohub creates an
unverified login if the holder does not already have one. The holder must verify
that email before using the pack.

At checkout the buyer accepts the current terms and asks to be able to use the
pack immediately, within the statutory withdrawal period. When the buyer
validly withdraws after requesting immediate use and a proportional charge
applies, the refund is the price paid after discounts multiplied by the unused
entries and divided by the total entries, rounded down to whole CZK. Past
visits and a visit booked for the day of withdrawal count as used. Later
bookings are cancelled and their entries count as unused.

The 14-day period for withdrawing online starts the day after the buyer
receives the purchase confirmation in Prague time. Under Czech Civil Code
§607, if day 14 is a Saturday, Sunday, or Czech public holiday, the period
runs through the end of the next Czech working day.
